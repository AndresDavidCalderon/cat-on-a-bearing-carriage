
At night, burglars enter your terrain (The fence is very easy to climb) and start milking your cows, then taking the bottles and putting them in a truck to prepare to go to another town. There are several trucks and collection points. 
The burglars take advantage of the darkness, sneak up to one of 10 cows and start miking them, you can stop the milking by hitting them with the car,  in which case the burglar will lift a white flag. If you do not succeed at that point, you can hit the burglar with the bat and recover the milk bottle, which will take up space in your inventory.

The inventory can be used to carry milk or weapons and traps brought from the weapon warehouse, though they cost coins. you get coins by stopping burglars and from delivery mode. 

Milk can be brought to safe storage, but will mean loosing precious time.  You can even go recover the milk from the trucks, but they are a bit too far and you risk the burglars using their tasers

The weapon warehouse is managed by a black cat with blue eyes, more weapons will unlock each night.

The game mode is presented through the first night, with no burglars to guide you through the procedures, in the tutorial the top left will have objectives.


Normally you win when surviving 90 seconds and loose or all 10 milk bottles are done.

The cat that manages the warehouse and leads you is called Miguel, has a ray scar and is black. He makes everything dramatic and more of a deal than it really is. He treats the milk thing like a world-saving operation. He is your friend and helps with operations on the farm because he is a bit awkward with humans.

The cat must be given a gun. Also zooming out the camera felt quite bad too. We need the baddies to appear on the map and other helpers, but not zooming out the camera

The thing with hitting them with the car itself is that if you have any speed requirement at all, it's going to be frustrating. So the solution is a Taser, the cat can Tase enemies if they are on a visible marked area (Which is small so that you still have to drift and stuff)

I've also gotta fix the remaining bottles indicator before evaluating the fun more.

The camera rotation makes this mode hard, along with there not being any map. There should be some details that help the player guide themselves. Maybe there should be only 1 pickup that moves.
